Who We Are

About Team Designated

Born from passion, built on precision — Team Designated is Pune's emerging premium event management company dedicated to crafting unforgettable experiences.

Our journey began in the corridors of MBA, where we took on the challenge of organizing large-scale college events. From managing logistics to curating immersive experiences for thousands of students — we delivered every single time. That track record of zero failures and 100% execution earned us our name.

Today, we've stepped into the open market — bringing that same relentless standard to corporate events, stand-up comedy nights, luxury weddings, exclusive parties, and Gen-Z experiences. Whether it's an intimate gathering or a sold-out show, Team Designated shows up — and shows out.
